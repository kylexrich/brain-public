# Clip Suggestions Criteria

A clip suggestion is a moment from the stream that could be published as **standalone content** — a YouTube Short, a tutorial, a highlight,
or a standalone video. It should make sense on its own without needing the full stream for context.

## What makes a good clip

- **Has a clear start and end** — a self-contained moment, not a random excerpt
- **Stands on its own** — a viewer who didn't watch the stream would still get value
- **Has a logical starting point** — ideally a hook, but at minimum a clear entry point that doesn't feel like jumping into the middle of
  something
- **Showcases personality, skill, or insight** — not just "things that happened"

## Good examples

- A strong story or anecdote Kyle tells
- A sharp opinion or contrarian take
- A useful debugging walkthrough with a clear resolution
- A memorable failure or breakthrough moment
- A chess game highlight or turning point
- A concise explanation of a technical concept

## Bad examples

- Generic coding with no narrative arc
- Setup/configuration with no payoff
- Conversations where context from earlier in the stream is required
- Moments that are only interesting if you know the full backstory

## Format categories

| Category    | Description                                          |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| `SHORT`     | Under 60 seconds, punchy, vertical-friendly          |
| `HIGHLIGHT` | A standout moment (1-5 min)                          |
| `TUTORIAL`  | Teaching or explaining something useful (any length) |
| `STORY`     | A narrative with setup, tension, and resolution      |
