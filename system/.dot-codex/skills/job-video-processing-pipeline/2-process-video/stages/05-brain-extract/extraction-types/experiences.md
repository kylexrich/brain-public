# Extraction Type: Experiences

## What Qualifies

Things that happened and what Kyle took from them. One concrete event per candidate — something Kyle explicitly narrated or clearly lived
through on stream.

## Extraction Constraints

| Field             | Allowed            |
|-------------------|--------------------|
| `extraction_type` | `direct` only      |
| `confidence`      | `high` or `medium` |

No composite experiences by blending multiple moments.

## Quality Criteria

- Must describe a concrete event or situation
- Must include what Kyle took from it (the lesson or takeaway)
- One experience per candidate

## What to Reject

- Composite experiences blending multiple moments
- Vague summaries without a specific event
- Events Kyle mentioned secondhand without personal involvement

## Destination

- Folder: `vault/experiences/`
- Frontmatter: `status: budding`, `creation_source: stream_extraction`, `extraction_type: direct`
