# Composite Clip Suggestions Criteria

A composite clip suggestion is a **multi-segment compilation** — several moments from the stream that, when stitched together, tell a story,
explore a theme, or create a compelling compilation.

Unlike individual clip suggestions, composites don't need each segment to stand alone. The value comes from the combination.

## What makes a good composite

- **Has a narrative thread** — the segments connect in a way that creates a journey, progression, or theme
- **Greater than the sum of parts** — watching the segments together adds meaning that individual clips wouldn't have
- **Has natural segment roles** — ideally an intro that draws you in, a body that develops the thread, and a payoff that resolves it. But at
  minimum, a logical starting point and a clear progression.

## Format categories

| Category            | Description                                                                   |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `GAME_RECAP`        | Round-by-round or match summary (e.g., a chess game from opening to analysis) |
| `TOPIC_DEEP_DIVE`   | Extended exploration of one theme across multiple stream moments              |
| `JOURNEY_ARC`       | Progression narrative — failure to success, learning curve, skill development |
| `THEME_COMPILATION` | Collection of moments on a similar topic or mood                              |
| `TOPIC_MERGE`       | Multiple related topics woven into one cohesive narrative                     |

## Editorial effort

| Level    | Meaning                                                                                    |
|----------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `LOW`    | Segments can be concatenated with minimal cuts — natural transitions exist                 |
| `MEDIUM` | Some trimming, reordering, or transition work needed between segments                      |
| `HIGH`   | Significant editing required — segments need context bridges, narration, or heavy trimming |

## Segment roles

| Role     | Position | Purpose                                                |
|----------|----------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| `INTRO`  | First    | Draws the viewer in, sets up the premise               |
| `BODY`   | Middle   | Develops the thread, builds tension or depth           |
| `PAYOFF` | Last     | Resolves, delivers the insight, or lands the punchline |
