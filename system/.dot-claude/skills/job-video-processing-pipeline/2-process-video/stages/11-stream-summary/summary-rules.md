# Summary Rules

Rules for generating the stream summary that appears in the YouTube description.

## Format

- **2-4 sentences**
- **Under 500 characters**
- Plain text only — no quotes, JSON, markdown, labels, or explanation

## Content

- Focus on the real stream arc — what Kyle worked on, what happened, memorable problems or breakthroughs
- Be informative and specific — a viewer should get a clear sense of what the stream covered
- Ground everything in actual transcript content

## Tone

- Engaging but grounded
- Authentic to the stream's actual events
- No hype, clickbait, or unsupported claims

## Do NOT include

- Generic filler ("In today's stream, Kyle...")
- Mention that the description was AI-generated
- Names of external people (see [privacy-rules.md](../../shared/privacy-rules.md))
- Sensitive information (see [privacy-rules.md](../../shared/privacy-rules.md))

## Examples

Good:
> Kyle shipped a redesigned EMLY widget icon on Day 5 — swapping out the "Talk to Emily" button for a circular gradient icon with an
> animated sound-wave logo, then raced to deploy it to production before a 1pm client call.

> Kyle returned from a system crash (128GB RAM exhausted by too many spawned sub-agents) and picked up where he left off: finalizing brand
> guidelines for EXP, reviewing a co-founder's CRM integration PR, and polishing the EMLY landing page hero section spacing.

Bad:
> Kyle had another productive stream working on various projects and making good progress on his startup journey.
